A controlled workflow

Reuse approved work. Refuse unsupported claims.

The builder organises what the client has explicitly approved and separates anything stale, contradictory, or missing before delivery.

1

Bring structured inputs

Load a JSON claim profile, CSV questionnaire, and UTF-8 Markdown evidence records on the local machine. PDF, Word, and image extraction is not yet supported.

2

Bind claims to support

Match draft language to stable fact and evidence IDs. Contradictions and stale material are separated.

3

Hand off editable files

Deliver an Excel review workbook, Word security overview, cited answer library, evidence index, gap register, and hashes.

Non-negotiable boundaries

Preparation support—not automated assurance.

Trust comes from showing uncertainty, not hiding it behind fluent text.

What it is designed to do

  • Keep sensitive review material local
  • Reuse customer-approved wording consistently
  • Cite facts and evidence beside each draft
  • Expose unsupported and conflicting claims
  • Record exact source hashes for later comparison

What it will not do

  • Invent controls, certifications, or operating history
  • Treat a policy document as proof of effectiveness
  • Guarantee compliance, acceptance, or sales outcomes
  • Give legal advice or submit responses unattended
  • Upload evidence or add hidden telemetry
